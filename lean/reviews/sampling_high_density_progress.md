# Measurable sampling and return to original cells

Three new modules close the local sampling application using actual measurable data. They contain six source theorems; the complete source audit reports15 local declarations/theorems including generated helpers. Every dependency is a standard foundation, with no custom axiom or admitted proof. Sources are frozen in the checkpoint15 snapshot.

## Constructed outcome and exact budgets

SamplingMeasurablePivot.low_or_pivot fixes the sampling scale cutoff and pivot constant before all configurations. Its inputs are the original measurable full/marked sets, carrier containment, comparable physical masses, marked mass, separated axes, real-cap data, physical two ends, almost-everywhere marked broadness, and fixed logarithmic conditioning budgets. The normalized row probabilities, threshold, high set, dyadic test depth and actual simultaneous outcome are constructed internally through SamplingMeasurableAssembly.

The SAME normalized probabilities and SAME outcome supply the sharp2/3–4/3 full-density interval and the full SampleGood record. The proved angular radius lower bound is inverted into the literal logarithmic budget used by pivot. The low alternative is bounded by the cardinality of the same actual positive-measure support, via its finite subtype. No expected mass, good outcome, energy bound, or requested union estimate is an input.

## Both alternatives give one bound

SamplingHighDensity.construct combines the actual high-cell pivot with LowCellAbsorption.uniform_low_cap_bound. The latter absorbs the marked-fraction and cutoff logarithms using the positive margin D<m+1 and density power C>=1. Here D=(2m+3+d′)/4 and C=(p+2q+4)/4; p>=1 and q>=2 imply C>=1. The low coefficient is exactly1/(2*ratio*cHigh), where ratio=C0/min(c0,1) and cHigh is the dimensional threshold coefficient.

Taking the minimum of the two positive constants, BEFORE the actual configuration, gives

`c A⁻¹ δ^(m−D+ε) λ^C M ≤ #positiveSupport`.

The source high-density cutoff λ>δ^(1/3) is covered by the non-strict formal hypothesis λ>=(1/δ)^(-1/3). Original broadness and full two ends remain explicit. The theorem permits every positive original two-ends exponent: input_weaken_alpha changes only the exponent to min(alpha,1/4), preserving all actual full and marked sets, and all_exponents applies the construction to that weakened input.

## Actual original-grid conclusion

SamplingOriginalCells.construct applies the preceding result to a measurable configuration at mesh δ/τ. Each actual full shading must lie in the literal common pieceMap image of an original finite cell union S. The previously proved positive_support_card constructs the upper bound

`#positiveSupport ≤ (2*(k+2)+3)^(k+2) * #S`

in ambient dimension k+2, for0<τ<=1 and common width factor W>=1. Dividing the fixed estimate constant by this dimensional factor yields

`c A⁻¹ (δ/τ)^(m−D+ε) λ^C M ≤ #S`.

There is no extra inverse-τ factor, no identification with transformed normalized volume, and no overlap assertion about pulled-back sampled cubes. The only input support condition is literal subset containment, which the actual angular/spatial normalization is designed to construct.
