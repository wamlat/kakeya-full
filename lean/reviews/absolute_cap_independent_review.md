Reviewed the final source statements and proof bodies of AbsoluteCapEstimates and AbsoluteCapGlobalization and their concrete unchanged-scale selection interface. No mathematical defect found in the stated scope.

This is an interface-completion issue, not a flaw in these theorem proofs.
