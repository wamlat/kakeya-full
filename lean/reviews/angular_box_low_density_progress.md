`AngularBoxLowDensity.lean` completes the actual application of the frozen local hairbrush low-density bound to every original good spatial box of the SAME `AngularSpatialSampling.Package U`. It does not select a different output, spatial group, segment, density class, or color.

Its three theorems prove the actual spatial overlap constant is at least one, prove the literal final box broadness constant is at least one, and prove the final uniform bound. No `max 1` modification of K or of marked sets is used.

## Uniform statement

`construct` fixes a positive c before the original family F, angular pieces P, refinement V, simultaneous package U, and good box q. It concludes

`c*A^(-1)*(δ/P.tau)^(m-D+ε)*(physicalDensity V width)^C*originalBoxPopulation(q) ≤ #refinedCells(V,width,q)`.

The physical density and box population are the original ones BEFORE the joint segment/color/measured selection. The tested low-density value is exactly `(U.output q).density`.

The fixed hypotheses are positive alpha/beta, original B≥1, m≥1, C≥2, ε>0, a≥1, the positive sparse margin `(m+3)/2-D+(C-2)/3`, original base bound R≥0, and the explicit normalization `2≤widthFactor(k+2,width)^(k+2)`. The original family must satisfy its actual admissibility, separation, base bound, and cap bound with A≥1. The actual remaining case tests are

- `(δ/P.tau)^a≤δ`;
- `(U.output q).density≤(δ/P.tau)^(1/3)`;
- `1≤log(2/δ)` for the already constructed actual refinement budgets.

There is no new transformed small-scale cutoff. The positivity δ>0, original density≤1, and membership of the original angular group in P.groups are explicit.

## Inputs discharged internally

The wrapper obtains original Full/Marks measurability, nestedness, physical tube carriers, separation and cap control, and the angular aperture 3 from the original refinement and spatial package. It obtains actual per-tube upper full mass and per-box marked mass from the exact common physical normalization and `U.spatial.box_marked_mass`. Broadness comes from `U.spatial.broad`; two ends comes from the unchanged actual reference full shadings. The old support is the literal `box_full_support`, hence the conclusion counts precisely `refinedCells` rather than newly sampled cells.

All conditioning coefficients are fixed before configurations. It uses `AngularSpatialBudgets` with effective-retention exponent 1, two-ends exponent 1, broadness exponent 2, and the marked-ratio budget divided by four with exponent 1. Thus the local hairbrush theorem receives only actual geometric data and already proved numerical conditioning; no marked mass, energy, expected-value, support-cardinality, or desired lower-count premise remains to be supplied per box.
