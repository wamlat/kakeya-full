# Independent review: LebesgueMaximal

Read-only review of `LebesgueMaximal.lean`, SHA-256 `d5e0eb9c25e731e854835d7bb28647e1f336888e15a1439d8ed180bb5cb705b9`, by `/root/finite_formal`. The entire source and the corresponding `MaximalShading.Estimate`/`MaximalLengths.Estimate` predicates were inspected. I also checked the pinned Mathlib definition of `NullMeasurableSet` and the actual construction in `NullMeasurableSet.exists_measurable_subset_ae_eq`.

**No mathematical or statement-linkage defect found.** The new predicates extend the old Borel predicates to null-measurable sets for Euclidean volume, which is the appropriate completed-measure scope. The forward and reverse implications establish equivalence, not an additional analytic assumption.

`of_borel` selects an actual Borel subset Z_i of each original Y_i with Z_i=Y_i almost everywhere. The subset relation is literal, so the same original carrier contains Z_i. `measureReal_congr` preserves every original density lower bound exactly, and the finite-index union is almost-everywhere equal by `countable_iUnion`; its original union volume is therefore exactly the one on the right of the final inequality. It never uses a measurable superset to claim original geometric support. The constant is literally the old c, chosen before the family, positions, scale, density and representatives.

`lengths_of_borel` repeats the same argument with the actual original variable-length carrier volumes and fixed width. It preserves individual lengths, both length bounds, directions, separation, row density and the literal sum of tube volumes. It adds no bounded-position, cap, two-ends, dimension-positivity or comparison premise. The two reverse implications use only Borel implies null-measurable. Empty families and formally infeasible length intervals do not require division or an existence assumption.

All endpoint wrappers use the corresponding proved old predicates: general n≥6 profile, its radical formula, the six-dimensional 33/8 first step, six/eight radical endpoints and 29/7,37/7. `six_first_step` is an implication with an unused base because the stronger unconditional conclusion is available; it does not assume that a numerical exponent identity proves a geometric transition. These are maximal-*shading* predicates, not operator-norm assertions. This module neither declares nor needs a new analytic axiom.

**Boundary:** this closes the completed-measure input range for the original-position maximal-shading conclusions and their bounded-length versions. It does not itself extend the separate sampling or angular-decomposition Input records; those need their own nested full/marked representative adapters.
