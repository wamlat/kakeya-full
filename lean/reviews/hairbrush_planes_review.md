# Independent review: HairbrushPlanes

Reviewed the current HairbrushPlanes.lean independently after AngularDecomposition. No geometric, exponent or quantifier defect was found. No edits were made.

The transverse-coordinate map is the tail projection after an actual linear isometry. Its norm bound is the Euclidean orthogonal projection bound, and `plank_iff_affine_plane` proves the exact equivalence with distance to the affine span of the stem axis and the supplied transverse vector. The parameter named normal is the transverse spanning direction of the plane, rather than an orthogonal normal to that plane; this naming does not affect the definition or proof. The closest-stem-point theorem proves that transverse norm is the actual distance to the full stem line, not merely a convenient coordinate proxy.

The direction-cap bound for a point in an err-thick plank uses s≤transverse radius and2err≤s to show that its signed scalar coordinate has magnitude at least s/2. The resulting projective error4err/s accounts correctly for orientation and normalization. Plane-bin overlap uses separation of the transverse unit representatives, which live in dimension ambient−1. Applying spherical packing there yields exponent ambient−2, as required. It never assumes that all original tube directions lie on that lower-dimensional sphere. Its requirements delta>0, C≥1, 0<s≤1 and2Cdelta≤s are explicit and justify the cap's admissible scale.

The finite plane-bin net is constructed from actual original representatives. Projective closeness is converted to a signed scalar match, so no global orientation choice is hidden. Inside the radius-R region the enlargement err+(R+err)delta is correctly bounded by (2C+R)delta when err=Cdelta and delta≤1. A negative R merely makes the bounded-region premise empty; no positive-radius assertion is inferred from it.

For actual bristles meeting the stem, a common carrier point gives two axis centers at distance≤2delta. Every bristle point then has transverse distance≤3delta from its own actual stem plane. This is proved directly with carrier witnesses, including endpoint caps. It does not assume exact line intersection. The construction remains meaningful for a parallel bristle because unitize(0)=0; the later unit-representative theorem explicitly excludes that degenerate case via positive projective angle. The chord-to-transverse lower bound is a valid deliberately loose factor-two consequence of the unit sphere identity. The final bristle bins have thickness(6+R)delta and count≤C*delta^(−(ambient−2)), with the required bounded-region and nonparallel premises visible.

These results establish actual plane containment, finite bins and pointwise bin overlap away from the stem. They do not yet establish the planar-shell energy bound, measurable shell retention, or the global analytic hairbrush estimate. Those are separate downstream obligations, not unstated assumptions inside the reviewed lemmas.
