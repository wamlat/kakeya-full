# Actual measurable angular-spatial decomposition, unit-axis checkpoint

Three new modules compile with zero diagnostics, and their exact-source full-environment audits pass with only standard Lean foundations (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). No custom axiom, sorry, selection/count/energy oracle, bounded-position hypothesis, density hypothesis or two-ends hypothesis is used.

`SpatialTubeCover` assigns an actual transverse 2*tau lattice label and longitudinal unit-lattice label to every original tube. The longitudinal label closes the arbitrary-position finite-length cover explicitly. For a direction in a 3*tau cap, every width*delta carrier is contained in one translated parallel box with half-length 2+width and transverse radius (k+width+3)*tau, in ambient k+1. That box is inside the literal lengthCarrier of an explicitly constructed unit-direction tube, with length 2*(2+width). At an actual point, the number of distinct boxes is at most Csp=(2*ceil(2+width)+3)*(2*ceil((k+width+3)/2)+3)^k. This comes from two actual lattice counts and a product injection.

`MeasurableAngularSpatial` constructs the subfamilies by compressing each actual angular piece to its nonempty original indices, then partitioning its actual spatial labels. Its original-index maps are injective within each group and prove disjointness across distinct angular/spatial groups. All final shadings are actual measurable subsets of their own original shading; unions remain in the original union. Entire original tube carriers, rather than only shading supports, have the containing-box/tube field. Direction separation is inherited exactly when assumed. Measured spatial restoration retains 3/4 of the actual angular mass and transfers pointwise broadness. Actual final subunion overlap is at most 2*Csp*tau^(-beta).

`MeasurableAngularDecomposition.construct` creates the angular pieces internally, including the empty-family case. It proves the source natural-log mass retention c_k*log(2/delta)^(-3), with c_k=9*(log2)^3/(32*Cang), Cang=packingConstant(k)*3^k. The sharper underlying mass is 9/[32*Cang*(J+1)]. The proof uses log(2/delta)>=log2, not an unstated log>=1 assumption. The broadness error is 64*Cang*Csp, uniform for beta in [0,1]. All constants are explicit functions of dimension and fixed width, preceding scale, population, actual shadings, directions, positions and beta.

This closes the measurable assertions of source Lemma 3.2 under literal unit-axis carriers, with arbitrary fixed width and arbitrary positions. The exact raw families/shadings have finite dependent angular/spatial indices; their nested overlap sum is the literal number of occupied output groups.
