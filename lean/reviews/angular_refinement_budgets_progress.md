# Actual angular and spatial logarithmic budgets

Two new modules derive fixed natural-logarithm budgets from actual angular pieces and actual refined reference shadings. Both compile with zero diagnostics and pass complete local-declaration audits with standard foundations only.

With L=log(2/delta), the proved seed-log comparison gives eta(P)>=etaCoefficient(k)*L^-1. The actual refined depth is at most depthFactor(k)*L once L>=1. Actual marked incidence then gives V.N>=[etaCoefficient/(32*depthFactor)]*L^-2*activeGroupCount, and physicalDensity>=[etaCoefficient/(2*W^ambient)]*L^-1*lambdaOriginal.

For every actual good spatial box, the literal effective selection fraction has a fixed lower L^-1 budget. Its original full two-ends coefficient has an upper L budget and marked broadness has an upper L^2 budget. Every geometric factor, spatial overlap coefficient, separation palette and segment count remains in the fixed constants.

The sum of all good-box tube populations in one angular group is at least [etaCoefficient/(512*depthFactor^2)]*L^-3*activeGroupCount. Summing over the actual retained angular groups and invoking the proved original pruning yields a fixed positive coefficient times L^-4*MOriginal. All sufficiently marked boxes are included; no single-box selection introduces a scale-dependent population loss.

The proofs do not assume comparable logs at different scales. L>=1 is explicit, and the high-density application supplies this through its fixed small-scale cutoff; bounded scales need a separate case. These are quantitative outputs of actual Pieces/Refinement/SpatialData constructions, not a replacement for invoking those constructors in the final theorem.
